How it works · VPPR methodology

BIVO programs and adapts. You just supervise.

Built on VPPR, the methodology Toni Bota uses with professional players.
  1. V

    Assess

    Each athlete completes their profile in the app: injuries, mobility, level, equipment, available days and goals.

  2. P

    Prioritize

    BIVO identifies what each athlete needs to work on to perform and prevent injuries in their sport.

  3. P

    Program

    Builds their personalized plan from over 1,200 racket-specific exercises.

  4. R

    Quantify and reprogram

    Tracks load, compliance and tests, and keeps readjusting the plan.

AI builds and adapts. You supervise, edit and decide. And you spend your time on what grows your business: selling.
